Cue · design review

Pick the look.
Same app, two personalities.

Every layout below is drawn twice from the same structure. The cheerful skin warms the palette, rounds the corners and lifts the shadows; the professional skin cools the palette, tightens the type and flattens the surfaces. Nothing moves except the mood — so you are choosing a tone, not a different app.

CheerfulCream background, coral + violet, 22–24 px radii, coloured shadows, heavier headings, gradient pills and a little sparkle.
ProfessionalCool grey, ink + indigo, 12–14 px radii, neutral shadows, tighter type, flat pills, borders instead of glow.
01

Gallery — three layouts

The home screen after the redesign: Recent, Screen recordings and MP3 music in one place. Same content in all three; what changes is density and where the tabs live.

A

Library

Three-column grid with counts in the tab strip and an extended Add button. Densest, most familiar, and the music tab becomes a real library.

B

Cinematic

A hero card for the last song and two-column artwork cards with a bottom navigation bar. The most expressive, the least information per screen.

C

Timeline

Date-grouped rows with wide thumbnails and a source chip, plus search in the header. The easiest to scan, with the fewest items visible at once.

02

Song details — two forms

One set of inputs, no repeated search boxes. The cover is the artwork entry point, secondary fields collapse, and the clip and quality controls stay in reach of the Create button.

F1

Details-first

Compact cover beside inline title and artist, a single match card, then clip and quality. The whole form fits with barely any scrolling.

F2

Cover-first

A large cover with the title over it, then tidy cards for fields, clip and quality. More scroll, more of a “now playing” feel.

03

Shared screens

Processing, the saved result and the new delete-source setting. These look the same in every layout, so they only depend on the skin.

P

Processing

One ring, one stage, one cancel. The delete-source promise is visible in the steps, not hidden in Settings.

R

Saved

The new MP3 with its cover, where it landed, and the actions you actually use. It confirms when the source recording was removed.

S

Settings — after processing

Delete-source lives here with a confirm switch, next to the identification toggles it depends on.
